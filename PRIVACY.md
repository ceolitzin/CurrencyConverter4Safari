# Privacy Policy

Currency Converter for Safari does not collect, store or share personal data.

- **Selected text** is read only when you select text on a page, and it is analyzed locally inside that page. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Network requests** go only to `open.er-api.com` (ExchangeRate-API) and, if that fails, `api.frankfurter.dev` (Frankfurter), to download a table of exchange rates against the US dollar. These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit. Those providers may log ordinary request metadata such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
- **Settings** (target currency and the meaning of `$`) and the cached rate table are stored locally with Safari's extension storage.
- There are no analytics, ads, trackers or accounts.

The extension asks for access to all websites only so it can show the conversion bubble wherever you select a price. You can limit it to specific sites in Safari → Settings → Extensions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ceolitzin/CurrencyConverter4Safari/issues
